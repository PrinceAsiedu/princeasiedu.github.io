About

Blackmarshal is the public engineering identity of Prince Asiedu.

I am a software engineer and open-source contributor based in Accra, Ghana. On this site I write and publish as Blackmarshal, a long-standing handle that is now the name of this website.

My work sits at the intersection of backend engineering and software supply chain security — how software is built, signed, verified, and trusted. That focus is grounded in theipxis homelab, a self-hosted DevSecOps lab I run on Debian with Podman, Gitea, Woodpecker CI, Zot, Authentik, OpenBao, and Ollama, and inPhelsy, a Django-based multi-vendor marketplace built for the realities of African commerce.

Beyond builds, I am a co-founder and operations lead of OSSAfrica, an open-source community connecting African developers to security practices that actually apply to their engineering work. The community is independent — it is no longer affiliated with OpenSSF or the OpenSSF BEAR Working Group, under which I previously led the OSSAfrica Special Interest Group (2024–2026).

This site is deliberately evidence-based: each project and claim is linkable to a repository, a piece of writing, or a role. Anything unverifiable is marked as such rather than presented as fact.
